CLASS SPHEREHEIST · PWANI UNIVERSITY

POLICY DRAFT · REVIEW BEFORE PUBLIC LAUNCH

Privacy information

This draft describes intended CLASS SPHERE handling and must be reviewed and completed with the responsible university/class contacts before public operation.

Information collected

Student registration uses full name, admission number, email address and a password hash. The service stores account status, session data, academic preferences and activity needed to provide the requested features. Contact form messages are stored for administrator review. Email campaign outcomes and administrative audit events may also be stored.

How information is used

Account details support sign-in, email verification, timetable and class notices. Student email addresses are available only to authorized administrators and are not shared with other recipients in campaign messages.

Public profiles and photographs

Student directory profiles are hidden by default and require administrator approval of a display name. Admission numbers and email addresses are not public. Photos are published only after appropriate permission is confirmed.

Resources, email and AI

Academic resources are intended for authenticated students and administrators. Non-essential bulk email requires recipient eligibility and includes an unsubscribe route. AI features are not enabled until a real provider is configured; provider, retention, cost and data handling must be disclosed before activation. Do not submit sensitive personal information to an AI feature.

Security and retention

The application uses hashed passwords, server-managed sessions, access checks and secure production transport. Retention periods, responsible contact details, complaint process, applicable institutional policy and jurisdiction-specific disclosures must be completed by the platform owner before production.
