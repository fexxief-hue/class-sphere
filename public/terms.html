Terms of use

These draft terms describe expected use of the class portal. The responsible platform owner must review and approve them before launch.

Use of the portal

Use your own account, keep your password private and provide accurate registration information. Do not attempt to access another user's account, profile, resources or messages.

Academic materials

Use uploaded notes, assignments and past papers for permitted educational purposes. Respect the rights and instructions attached to each resource. Do not redistribute material without permission.

Communications and uploads

Do not submit abusive, unlawful or confidential content through the contact form, announcements, email or uploads. Administrators may remove inappropriate content and suspend accounts under an approved class policy.

AI features

AI-generated assistance may contain errors and is not an official university decision or substitute for lecturer guidance. AI announcement drafts require administrator review before publication or email delivery.

Availability and policy contacts

Maintenance, account recovery, reporting contacts, content takedown, governing rules and limitation clauses must be completed by the responsible owner with appropriate institutional review before public operation.
